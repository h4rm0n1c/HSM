## Seed idea

HSM should not only compare AI systems to human memory in the abstract.

It should examine how humans maintain state with each other:

- trust formation
- reputation
- repeated interaction
- correction after mistakes
- shared memory
- witness testimony
- emotional attunement
- disagreement and repair
- friendship as a long-running state channel
- social integrity as an emergent property

Human integrity may often be inferred from patterns across time rather than verified by one explicit judge.

That may map to the emerging HSM idea that integrity is not a single module, but an emergent property of evidence, provenance, retrieval, contradiction handling, update discipline, and repeated correction.

## Memory questions for later

Explore:

- Why do some memories surface only after an emotional incident?
- What is the time delay between reaction and explanation?
- How do humans decide whether another human's later explanation is credible?
- How does sleep, exhaustion, or overload affect retrieval and narrative formation?
- How does hyperfocus distort perceived time?
- What does it mean when a person says they were operating on a different level earlier in the day?
- How do people use anchors to return to stable state?
- How do trusted humans help each other recover state?
